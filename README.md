# 🚀 HireLens AI – Your AI Career Coach

An AI-powered career acceleration platform designed to help students, freshers, and professionals improve their career readiness through intelligent resume analysis, skill gap detection, interview preparation, and personalized career guidance.

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://hirelens-ai-accelerator-39.lovable.app

---

## 📌 About the Project

HireLens AI is an intelligent career assistance platform that helps users understand their professional strengths, identify areas for improvement, and prepare for job opportunities.

The platform brings multiple career development tools together in one place, making the job preparation process smarter and more efficient.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. AI Resume Analyzer
- Analyze uploaded resumes.
- Evaluate resume content.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses.
- Provide suggestions for improvement.

### 2. Skill Gap Detection
- Identify missing skills.
- Analyze skill requirements.
- Highlight areas that need improvement.
- Support career development.

### 3. AI Interview Preparation
- Assist users with interview preparation.
- Provide interview-related guidance.
- Help users improve their readiness for job interviews.

### 4. AI Career Guidance
- Provide personalized career assistance.
- Help users explore career development opportunities.
- Offer intelligent career recommendations.

### 5. AI Chatbot Assistant
- Interactive career assistance.
- Answer career-related queries.
- Provide guidance throughout the preparation process.
